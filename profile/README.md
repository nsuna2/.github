## 👋 About us

**Nsuna** is a social travel and lifestyle platform. People use it to find unique hotels, apartments, restaurants and experiences, share them with friends, and book them right in the app.

> **Discover → Book → Scan → Enjoy**

## ✨ What we're building

| | Feature | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 🏨 | **Stays** | Unique hotels, apartments and experiences picked to suit each user |
| 🍽️ | **Restaurants** | Browse menus, book a table or order a meal in a few taps |
| 📰 | **Feed** | Discover, share and connect with friends and the community |
| 📱 | **Instant booking** | Book and check in with QR scanning |
